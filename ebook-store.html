CHAPTER 1

Understanding AI in Plain English

When people say “AI” in the context of everyday work, they usually mean a chat assistant: a program you type to in ordinary language, and which replies with text, ideas, summaries or images.

The important thing to grasp is that an AI assistant is not a search engine and not a person. It generates a response that fits your request. Most of the time that response is excellent. Occasionally it is confidently wrong. Knowing the difference is the most valuable skill in this book.

The best way to think about AI is as a very fast junior assistant. It can get you roughly eighty percent of the way to a finished piece in a couple of minutes. The last twenty percent is where you add your voice, your local knowledge and your accuracy checks.
